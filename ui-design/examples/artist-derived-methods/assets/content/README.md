# Generated content assets

These images were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generator for use inside the frontend method specimens. Each prompt translates documented visual principles into an original subject and composition; none asks for a reproduction of a known artwork. Text, UI chrome, logos, watermarks, and recognizable protected characters were excluded.

## Prompt set

An original threshold archive where paper records become roots, a brass key becomes a pale bird, and a fox-like witness observes. Fine surreal gouache and tempera, nested architecture, nocturnal green, bone, oxidized teal, pale gold, and one heated red accent.

### Fujiko F. Fujio visual narrative

An original child and seed-shaped weather-memory device in a familiar balcony garden. Crisp hand-inked editorial illustration, controlled asymmetric curves, flat mint, coral, lemon, teal, and off-white color. No protected characters, proprietary gadgets, or franchise color arrangements.

### Jackson Pollock

An original action-painting field with distinct line families shaped by viscosity, gravity, pooling, drag, interruption, and revision. Cyan, hot pink, acid yellow-green, and chalk white on charcoal, with uneven density and exposed ground.

### Lucian Freud

An original, dignified, fully clothed older woman seated by a window. The chair, floor, hands, posture, doorway, and room visibly share weight and pressure. Dense local color mixing, embodied brush structure, and varied edges.

### Lucio Fontana

An original contemporary gallery installation with a taut coral membrane, deep cobalt aperture, concealed warm backlight, and one moving visitor. Physically plausible architectural photography with material depth and a legible viewing route.

### Gustav Klimt

An original contemporary botanist portrait. A lifelike face and hands remain the visual anchor while leaves, broken rings, grid blooms, and seed motifs form a roaming mixed-media field in oxide green, black, brass, crimson, and parchment.

### Pieter Bruegel the Elder

An original high-view community garden immediately before heavy rain. Many small actions remain legible: carrying seedlings, repairing drainage, covering beds, sharing tools, guiding children, and checking clouds.

### Hans Holbein the Younger

An original formal portrait of a contemporary coastal mapping director with a calibration wheel, field chart, institute seal, precise hands, restrained expression, shallow space, and material-specific detail.

### Titian

An original rainy ferry departure at the irreversible second when a rope falls. Layered chromatic construction, opposed movement, readable hands and gangway, differentiated wet cloth, metal, skin, and water.

### Edvard Munch

An original winter shoreline separation scene with two figures moving in opposite directions, a rail that blocks connection, and a rope line that echoes distance. Matte blocks, controlled grain, and directional contours; no famous bridge, scream, or red-sky quotation.
