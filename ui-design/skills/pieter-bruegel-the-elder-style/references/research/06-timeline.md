# Pieter Bruegel the Elder: visual and documentary timeline

## Lifetime and oeuvre

| Date | Event / work | Evidence and significance |
|---|---|---|
| c. 1525-30 | Birth, probably in or near Breda; exact year and place uncertain. | **【权威二手】** Van Mander says a village called Bruegel near Breda; later institutional records retain uncertainty. [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/en/page/shadowy-life); [DBNL, Van Mander](https://www.dbnl.org/nieuws/text.php?id=mand001schi01) |
| 1540s | Reported training with Pieter Coecke van Aelst. | **【权威二手】** Based substantially on Van Mander; no studio notebook survives. [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/en/page/shadowy-life) |
| 1551 | Enters Antwerp Guild of Saint Luke as free master. | **【一手/官方】** Firm professional anchor. [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/en/page/shadowy-life) |
| 1552-54 | Travels through France and the Alps to Italy; works in Rome. | **【一手/官方 + 权威二手】** Surviving drawings and early biography support the journey; the Alps become a durable visual resource. [The Met, 2001 overview](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2001/pieter-bruegel-the-elder); [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/en/page/shadowy-life) |
| c. 1554-55 | Begins intensive collaboration with Hieronymus Cock in Antwerp. | **【一手/官方】** Landscapes launch a publisher-led print career. [Museum Mayer van den Bergh, “The print-maker”](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/en/page/print-maker) |
| 1555-56 | *Large Landscapes* issued after Bruegel's designs. | **【一手/官方】** Composite panoramic landscape becomes a reproducible public form. [The Met, catalogue](https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications/pieter-bruegel-the-elder-drawings-and-prints) |
| 1556-57 | Bruegel draws *Big Fish Eat Little Fish*; Cock publishes the engraving under Bosch's name in 1557. | **【一手/官方】** Key case of design authorship, market branding, and later attribution correction. [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/P_1875-0710-2651) |
| 1557-60 | Designs of the Seven Deadly Sins and Seven Virtues circulate in print. | **【一手/官方】** Proverbs, allegory, grotesque detail, and serial publication converge. [The Met, catalogue](https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications/pieter-bruegel-the-elder-drawings-and-prints) |
| 1559 | *Netherlandish Proverbs* and *The Fight between Carnival and Lent*. | **【一手/官方】** High-view simultaneous fields organize sayings, customs, and social behavior. [Berlin](https://search.smb.museum/object/obj-867614); [KHM](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/the-fight-between-carnival-and-lent-320) |
| 1560 | *Children's Games*; Bruegel personally etches *The Rabbit Hunt*. | **【一手/官方】** Encyclopedic crowd painting and the sole autograph print show two different line/control systems. [KHM](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/children-s-games-321); [The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/366870) |
| 1562-63 | *The Triumph of Death*; *Dulle Griet*; *The Tower of Babel*. | **【一手/官方】** Apocalyptic social totality, Boschian transformation, and an architectural system filled with labor. [Prado](https://www.museodelprado.es/en/the-collection/art-work/the-triumph-of-death/d3d82b0b-9bf2-4082-ab04-66ed53196ccc); [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/nl/pagina/dulle-griet); [KHM](https://www.khm.at/en/object/323/) |
| 1563 | Marries Mayken Coecke and settles in Brussels. | **【一手/官方】** Life transition is firm; patronage motive remains uncertain. [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/en/page/shadowy-life) |
| 1564 | *The Procession to Calvary*. | **【一手/官方】** Sacred narrative is submerged in Bruegel's largest surviving crowd and contemporary observation. [KHM](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/the-procession-to-calvary-324) |
| 1565 | Six-part Seasons cycle for Nicolaes Jongelinck; five panels survive. | **【一手/官方】** Peak integration of weather, labor, leisure, animal behavior, and landscape sequence. [KHM Seasons](https://zeitendernatur.khm.at/en/chapter-ii-the-seasons-a-la-bruegel/); [The Met, *The Harvesters*](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435809) |
| 1566 | *The Census at Bethlehem* and other populous religious scenes; Jongelinck provenance/inventory anchors major works. | **【一手/官方】** Contemporary village activity absorbs sacred history; copies later complicate states and attribution. [Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium, “Bruegel: The Originals”](https://fine-arts-museum.be/en/exhibitions/bruegel-the-originals); [National Gallery copy study](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/catalogues/campbell-2014/the-adoration-of-the-kings-2) |
| 1567 | *The Conversion of Paul*. | **【一手/官方】** Main event becomes small and embedded; a specific Alba allusion is not generally accepted by KHM. [KHM](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/the-conversion-of-paul-328) |
| 1568 | *Peasant Wedding*, *The Birdnester*, *The Misanthrope*, and other late works. | **【一手/官方】** Larger, nearer bodies coexist with compressed proverb and social observation. [KHM, *Peasant Wedding*](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/peasant-wedding-330); [KHM technical study](https://www.bruegel2018.at/en/revealing-glimpses-into-the-heart-of-panel-paintings/) |
| 1569 | Dies in Brussels and is buried at the Kapellekerk. | **【一手/官方】** End of a short career; Pieter the Younger is a child and Jan an infant. [ArchivIris](https://archiviris.be/fr/2019/08/25/1569-pierre-bruegel-meurt-a-bruxelles-et-entre-dans-lhistoire-1569-pieter-bruegel-sterft-in-brussel-en-ga-de-geschiedenis-in/); [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/en/page/shadowy-life) |
| 1570 | *Spring* from the Seasons print designs is published after his death. | **【一手/官方】** Graphic designs continue to enter circulation posthumously. [The Met, catalogue](https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications/pieter-bruegel-the-elder-drawings-and-prints) |

## Reception, copies, and attribution

| Date | Event | Evidence and significance |
|---|---|---|
| c. 1573 | Abraham Ortelius writes a memorial tribute in his *Album amicorum*. | **【权威二手】** Near-contemporary esteem within humanist circles; not Bruegel's own voice. [DBNL](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_vla010188901_01/_vla010188901_01_0013.php) |
| 1580s-1630s | Pieter Brueghel the Younger and family/workshop reproduce and adapt the Elder's compositions. | **【权威二手】** Copies may preserve earlier underdrawing states or lost edges, but also alter format, detail, and color. [National Gallery](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/catalogues/campbell-2014/the-adoration-of-the-kings-2) |
| 1604 | Karel van Mander publishes Bruegel's first substantial biography. | **【一手/官方：文献版本；权威二手：人物证词】** Essential early record, also a literary and theoretical construction. [DBNL](https://www.dbnl.org/nieuws/text.php?id=mand001schi01); [JHNA](https://jhna.org/articles/regurgitating-nature-celebrated-anecdote-by-karel-van-mander-about-pieter-bruegel-the-elder/) |
| 1894 | Fritz Mayer van den Bergh buys and identifies *Dulle Griet*, then poorly understood. | **【一手/官方】** Important modern rediscovery amid frequent Brueg(h)el misattribution. [Museum Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/nl/pagina/dulle-griet) |
| Early 1990s | Paper evidence removes a large group of Alpine drawings from Bruegel's oeuvre; other sheets are reconsidered. | **【一手/官方】** Major contraction and redefinition of graphic corpus. [The Met, 2001 overview](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2001/pieter-bruegel-the-elder) |
| 2001 | Met and Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen mount *Pieter Bruegel the Elder: Drawings and Prints*. | **【一手/官方】** Consolidates revised attribution and the centrality of graphic work. [The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2001/pieter-bruegel-the-elder) |
| 2012 | Royal Museums technical publication states the Brussels *Landscape with the Fall of Icarus* is not by Bruegel's hand, though its design is attributed to him. | **【权威二手】** Surviving object treated as copy after a lost original. [Royal Museums publication](https://fine-arts-museum.be/uploads/publications/files/Paysage_manieriste_26092012_pr05_2.pdf) |
| 2012-18 | KHM conducts systematic non-invasive examination of its twelve Bruegel panels. | **【一手/官方】** IR/IRR, X-ray, XRF, and 3D mapping reveal supports, pigments, underdrawing, and revisions. [KHM, “Researching Bruegel”](https://www.bruegel2018.at/en/researching-bruegel/) |
| 2018 | KHM's *Bruegel: The Hand of the Master* opens; Inside Bruegel launches. Prado completes major *Triumph of Death* restoration; *Dulle Griet* is also restored. | **【一手/官方】** Technical research corrects aged-color assumptions and exposes process. [KHM](https://www.bruegel2018.at/en/researching-bruegel/); [Prado](https://www.museodelprado.es/en/whats-on/multimedia/the-restoration-of-the-triumph-of-death-by-pieter/03215ac4-97b1-b431-221e-ed9336d574e2); [Mayer van den Bergh](https://museummayervandenbergh.be/nl/pagina/dulle-griet) |
| 9 Apr 2026 | Royal Museums and KIK-IRPA announce new non-invasive study of *Landscape with the Fall of Icarus*. | **【一手/官方】** Attribution, date, technique, and restoration remain under review. [Royal Museums](https://fine-arts-museum.be/en/news/new-technical-study-of-the-fall-of-icaru) |
| 2028 (scheduled) | Results of the new *Icarus* study are planned for a Bruegel exhibition. | **【一手/官方】** Future research milestone, not a result. [Royal Museums](https://fine-arts-museum.be/en/news/new-technical-study-of-the-fall-of-icaru) |

## Evolution summary

- **【研究推断】c. 1552-56:** observed and composite landscape becomes a graphic language. Evidence: [The Met, 2001 overview](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2001/pieter-bruegel-the-elder).
- **【研究推断】1556-60:** print-market allegory, proverb, grotesque, and large-panel encyclopedias develop in parallel. Evidence: [The Met, catalogue](https://www.metmuseum.org/met-publications/pieter-bruegel-the-elder-drawings-and-prints); [KHM, *Children's Games*](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/children-s-games-321).
- **【研究推断】1562-65:** architectural, apocalyptic, sacred, and seasonal “world pictures” integrate macro structure with micro action. Evidence: [KHM, *The Tower of Babel*](https://www.khm.at/en/object/323/); [KHM, Seasons](https://zeitendernatur.khm.at/en/chapter-ii-the-seasons-a-la-bruegel/).
- **【研究推断】1566-69:** sacred events remain embedded in contemporary society while several late works bring bodies and communal ritual closer. Evidence: [KHM, *The Conversion of Paul*](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/the-conversion-of-paul-328); [KHM, *Peasant Wedding*](https://www.khm.at/en/artworks/peasant-wedding-330).
- **【权威二手】After 1569:** print publication, family copying, collecting, restoration, and attribution repeatedly reshape which “Bruegel” viewers think they know. Evidence: [National Gallery copy study](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/catalogues/campbell-2014/the-adoration-of-the-kings-2); [The Met attribution history](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2001/pieter-bruegel-the-elder).

## Explicit non-events

- There are no “recent personal developments” after 1569.
- The 2026-28 entries concern scholarship and conservation, not activity by the historical artist.
- The timeline does not resolve the missing late-spring Seasons panel or the lost original proposed behind *Icarus*.
